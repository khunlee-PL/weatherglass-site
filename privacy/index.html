Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Weatherglass is free, has no ads, no subscriptions, no accounts, and no analytics or tracking. We do not run any server that receives your data. Almost everything the app does happens on your device, offline.

What the app stores on your device

Home positions you pin (a place name or a GPS point) are saved in your device's local storage so the app remembers them between sessions. They stay on your device. They are never uploaded to us or anyone else, and they are removed when you delete the pin or clear the app's data.

We do not collect, store, or have access to this information.

Location

The app asks for your location only when you choose to use it — when you tap to pin your current position, or to fetch weather or official warnings for where you are. When you do:

  • your approximate coordinates are sent to the weather service you requested (for example the U.S. National Weather Service, MET Norway, Meteoalarm, or Open-Meteo) solely to retrieve weather or warnings for that point;
  • the coordinates are used for that request and not stored by us (we have no server);
  • the app never accesses your location in the background and never tracks your movements.

You can decline the location permission and still use the whole offline atlas and search.

Third-party weather services

When you request live weather or warnings, the app contacts the relevant official or open weather service directly from your device. Those services receive the coordinates of your request and are governed by their own privacy terms:

  • U.S. National Weather Service — weather.gov/privacy
  • MET Norway — met.no privacy
  • Meteoalarm / EUMETNET — meteoalarm.org
  • Open-Meteo — open-meteo.com/en/terms

We share nothing with them beyond the request you initiate.

What we do not do

No advertising. No sale or sharing of personal data. No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. No user accounts. No background location. No collection of your data on any server we operate.

Children

The app collects no personal data, so it is safe for all ages. It is not directed at children in a way that collects information from them.
